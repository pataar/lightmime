Your Hue lights, following your screen.

lightmime syncs Philips Hue lights with your screen on Linux. The left of the picture goes to the lights left of your monitor or TV, the top to the lights above it, streamed over the Hue Entertainment API with low latency, the way Hue Sync does on other systems.

Install Quickstart Source on GitHub

Ambient light for the Linux desktop

One small Rust binary that pairs with your bridge, captures the screen and keeps the lights in step.

Made for Wayland

Capture goes through the desktop's screen-cast portal and PipeWire, on KDE Plasma, GNOME and wlroots compositors such as Sway and Hyprland. X11 works too. You pick the screen once.

A window and a tray icon

Pair with the bridge, choose an entertainment area and press Start. Tune brightness, smoothing and saturation while it runs; the tray starts and stops syncing.

Light on resources

Each frame is scaled down before it is touched. Syncing at 30 fps takes about 1 % of one CPU core and 36 MB of memory.

Headless if you like

lightmime run syncs without a window, for example as a systemd user service on a media PC.

Home Assistant, optionally

Turn on MQTT and lightmime shows up as a device with a sync switch, a status sensor and sliders for dashboards and automations.

Private and open source

It talks to your bridge on the local network and nothing else unless you enable MQTT. Dual-licensed under MIT or Apache-2.0.

How it works

  1. Capture

    The XDG ScreenCast portal hands lightmime a PipeWire stream of the screen you chose.

  2. Map

    Each light in the entertainment area gets the colour of the part of the screen where it stands.

  3. Stream

    Colours go to the bridge over encrypted DTLS, many times a second, smoothed to taste.

Install

lightmime has its own Flatpak repository, so updates arrive through flatpak update or your software centre.

$ flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists flathub https://dl.flathub.org/repo/flathub.flatpakrepo
$ flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists lightmime https://lightmime.pataar.nl/flatpak/lightmime.flatpakrepo
$ flatpak install --user lightmime io.github.pataar.lightmime

Prefer something else? There are release binaries for x86_64 and aarch64, cargo install --locked lightmime, and building from source. Then follow the Quickstart: pair, choose an area, press Start.

What you need

  • A Philips Hue bridge (the square, second-generation one) with an entertainment area set up in the Hue app.
  • A Linux desktop with PipeWire and xdg-desktop-portal with a screen-casting backend. Most current desktops have both.
